Step 2

Upload the schedule

Choose any Excel file (.xlsx) or CSV file with the school schedule. It can be the Individual Teacher's Program, a list with one class period per row (teacher, subject, grade, section, day, start, end), or one timetable per teacher. You see a preview first, and nothing is saved until you press Import. After that you can edit every period in the By teacher and By section tabs.

Delete accounts

Remove many accounts at once. The uploaded school schedule stays, and each teacher's periods are ready again when they sign up.

This clears the accounts in Attendly. Email and password logins stay in Supabase (Authentication, Users) until you delete them there.

Emails are sent through Brevo from the verified sender. Email is not a fully private channel, so use a Gmail the guidance office controls.

    Columns: Subject, Grade, Section, Day, Start, End. For a combined class write both sections in one cell, like Beethoven/Mozart. Every row becomes a subject automatically.
