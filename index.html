    Upload your schedule (Excel file, or the school class program)

    Columns: Subject, Grade, Section, Day, Start, End. For a combined class write both sections in one cell, like Beethoven/Mozart. Every row becomes a subject automatically. You can also upload the school's Teachers' Schedule (Individual Teacher's Program) or General Class Program as it is: Attendly finds your name in it (your profile name must match the file, like Zsa Mungcal), and fills in your grades, sections, days and times for each term.
