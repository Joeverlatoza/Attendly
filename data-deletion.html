Delete your Attendly data

To delete your account and all information Attendly holds about you:

  1. Send an email to Jjalatoza@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Attendly data".
  2. Send it from the email address you used to sign up, and include your name and school ID number.
  3. We will delete your account, profile and attendance records within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Attendly from your Facebook account under Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites.
