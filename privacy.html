Attendly Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Attendly is a school classroom attendance app. This page explains what information it uses.

What we collect

When you sign up or log in with email, Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address. Attendly also stores your school ID number, class or section, and your attendance records (when you were marked present).

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to log you in, show your attendance, and let teachers record attendance. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored securely with our hosting and database provider, Supabase. Google and Facebook only tell us that you signed in; we never see your Google or Facebook password.

Sharing

Your attendance information is visible to teachers and school administrators who use Attendly. We do not share it with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time. See how to delete your data.
